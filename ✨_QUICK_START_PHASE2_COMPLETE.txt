THE 8 STATUSES:

  1️⃣  📅 SCHEDULED   - Appointment created, awaiting confirmation
  2️⃣  ✅ CONFIRMED   - Patient confirmed attendance
  3️⃣  📍 CHECKED_IN  - Patient at reception, data validated
  4️⃣  ⏳ WAITING     - In queue, waiting for professional
  5️⃣  🔄 IN_PROGRESS - Currently being served
  6️⃣  ✔️ COMPLETED   - Service finished (AUTO-BILLING!)
  7️⃣  🚫 CANCELLED   - Cancelled appointment (NO BILLING)
  8️⃣  ❌ NO_SHOW     - Patient didn't show up (NO BILLING)
