THE 8 STATUSES AT A GLANCE:

  1. 📅 SCHEDULED    → Initial state (awaiting confirmation)
  2. ✅ CONFIRMED    → Patient confirmed
  3. 📍 CHECKED_IN   → At reception (unlocks reception!)
  4. ⏳ WAITING      → In queue
  5. 🔄 IN_PROGRESS  → Being served
  6. ✔️ COMPLETED    → Done (AUTO-BILLS!)
  7. 🚫 CANCELLED    → Cancelled (never bills)
  8. ❌ NO_SHOW      → Didn't show (never bills)
